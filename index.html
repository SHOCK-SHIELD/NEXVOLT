Why ShockShield?

Built for communities, designed for impact

Instant Hazard Reporting

Snap a photo, add location, and report electrical dangers in seconds. No account needed for emergency reports.

Live Hazard Map

Real-time visualization of all reported hazards with clustering, heatmaps, and status filtering.

Community Hype System

Neighbors amplify urgent reports. Top-voted hazards rise automatically for responder priority queue.

Voice Hazard Reports

Record voice notes to describe hazards hands-free. Perfect for dangerous or hard-to-reach situations.

Rewards & Leaderboards

Responder Dashboard

Municipal tools for dispatching teams, tracking resolutions, and managing priority queues efficiently.

How It Works

Simple steps to protect your community

01

Spot & Snap

See exposed wires, sparking transformers, or illegal connections? Open the app and take a photo.

02

Auto-Locate & Report

GPS pins your location instantly. Add a voice note or select hazard type. Anonymous option available.

03

Community Amplifies

Neighbors "hype" urgent reports. Top reports rise in priority queue for immediate responder attention.

04

Track Resolution

Watch real-time status: Reported → Under Review → Dispatched → Resolved. Earn credits for each step.

Hazard Categories

Know what to report

⚡

Exposed Live Wires

Bare conductors in streets, parks, or buildings. Highest risk of electrocution.

Critical
🔥

Transformer Burst

Exploding, leaking, or smoking distribution transformers. Fire and outage risk.

High
🔌

Electricity Theft

Illegal hooking, meter bypassing, or unauthorized consumption. Revenue loss.

Medium
⚠️

Illegal Sources

Unauthorized generators, solar backfeed, or unpermitted connections.

Medium
💡

Streetlight Fault

Flickering, dead, or sparking public lighting. Safety hazard at night.

Low
🏭

Substation Issue

Fence damage, oil leaks, or unusual noise from substations.

High
🌳

Vegetation Encroachment

Trees or branches touching power lines. Storm outage risk.

Medium
🔧

Equipment Damage

Broken insulators, cross-arms, or hardware on poles.

Medium
